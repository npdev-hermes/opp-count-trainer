Table rules & practice notes

S17 · US peek · 3:2 natural blackjack · double after split · up to four player hands. Split equal ranks only; split aces get one card and cannot re-split. Split 21 is not blackjack. No insurance, surrender, or burn card. Two automatic players hit until 17, without splitting or doubling; they do not use optimal basic strategy. Dealer completes its hand for counting practice even if everyone busts. Cut at 75% dealt; a conservative safety reserve may cause an earlier shuffle between rounds. Shuffling mid-hand voids that practice round and returns the stake. Your hand, shoe, balance, and history are saved on this device. Practice chips only; no guarantee of real-world profit.
