THE METHOD

One Per Person, developed by Carlos Zilzer. This trainer uses the Easy OPP convention: begin at +6; when a hand is FINAL, count the number of 2–6 cards and subtract 1. Apply it to every player hand, every simulated seat, and the dealer. Tens, faces and aces are always zero.

OPP is a running count—not true count and not Hi-Lo. The TC field above is a normalized optional exercise only: (RC − 6) ÷ exact decks remaining, to one decimal. It is not official OPP and never a betting signal.

Table rules & trainer notes

Six-deck shoe by default; selectable 2 / 6 / 8 decks. Cut card reshuffles at 25% remaining. S17, US peek, 3:2 blackjack, double any first two cards, double after split, max four hands. Split aces receive one card only and cannot be re-split. No surrender. No insurance. Cards stay visible after resolution so you can audit the count. Two simulated seats hit until 17 (not optimal basic strategy); they do not split or double. The dealer always completes its hand for counting practice, even when all players bust. No burn card. A safety reserve can trigger an earlier between-round shuffle in short shoes. Split hands replace the original hand in the count. Practice only: no guarantee of profit.
