Enter the count before the next deal. OPP is a running-count system: start at +6, then each final hand is low cards (2–6) minus one.

OPP, in one minute

THE METHOD

One Per Person, developed by Carlos Zilzer. Begin at +6; when a hand is FINAL, count the number of 2–6 cards and subtract 1. Apply it to every player hand, every simulated seat, and the dealer.

OPP is a running count—not true count and not Hi-Lo. The TC field is an optional normalized exercise only.

Table rules & trainer notes

Six-deck shoe by default; selectable 2 / 6 / 8 decks. Cut card reshuffles at 25% remaining. S17, US peek, 3:2 blackjack, double any first two cards, double after split, max four hands. Split aces receive one card only and cannot be re-split. No surrender or insurance. Cards stay visible after resolution so you can audit the count. Two simulated seats hit until 17. Practice only: no real money.
